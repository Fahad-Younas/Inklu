Browse all placesHow scoring works

Every place gets one of four ratings

  • 85–100%Fully accessible
  • 65–84%Mostly accessible
  • 40–64%Partly accessible
  • Below 40%Limited access
Score = Yes ÷ (Yes + No) × 100

    Built for how people actually get around

    The same 30 checks cover what matters to different visitors.

    Wheelchair and mobility aid users

    Step-free entrances, ramps, lifts, door widths and accessible toilets.

    People with low vision

    High-contrast signs, information in more than one format and staff who offer help.

    Families and older adults

    Rest points, seating, parking close to the entrance and a safe drop-off.
