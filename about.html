About INKLU

INKLU helps people with disabilities, older adults and families with prams find places that work for them, before they leave home.

Too often, access information is missing, out of date or wrong. We visit places in person, check them against one clear checklist and publish an honest score.

What we do

Visit

Two auditors visit each place and test its entrance, washrooms, parking, staff and information.

Score

Every place gets a score and a rating, so you can compare places at a glance.

Share

Search INKLU, open a place and see exactly which features work and which are barriers.

Our promise

We report what we find, including the barriers. A high score is earned, never bought.
