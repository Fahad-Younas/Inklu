The INKLU Accessibility Audit

Every place on INKLU is checked in person against 30 indicators in five areas. Each indicator is marked Yes, No or N/A.

What we check

Physical access

Step-free entrance, ramps, doors, paths, lifts and rest points.

Washrooms

Accessible toilet, turning space, grab bars, alarm and cleanliness.

Parking and transport

Accessible bays, drop-off points and transport options.

Staff and service

Trained staff, help on request, and counters usable when seated.

Information

Clear signs, online details and a way to ask access questions.

How scoring works

Score % = Yes ÷ (Yes + No) × 100. N/A items are left out. The score sets the rating:

85–100%

Fully accessible

65–84%

Mostly accessible

40–64%

Partly accessible

Below 40%

Limited access

Critical rule: if the step-free route to the main entrance or the accessible toilet is marked No, the rating cannot be higher than Partly accessible.
